How to read the three tabs

These are alternative futures, not a sum — bets on which sector Aomi wins first. Every number is priced through the same four equations as the calculator above; only the top-down volumes differ, and the billing policy is shared live.

The sector you win decides your lever mix and where the org points: wallet is an outcome business (partners BYOK, so managed-model margin is thin — chase MAU and retail flow), asset management is almost pure outcome (chase AUM and rich bps — highest revenue-per-user), and the platform is a hosting annuity with real managed-model upside (chase app count; lazy builders default to Aomi's key) — read it straight off the donuts and the fingerprint bars.

Two dials decide everything: Aomi share of outcome fee (the policy knob above — set it to 50% and watch every outcome slice swell) and the BYOK rate per sector (baked into managed turns/user — the reason wallet's model slice is thin and the platform's is not).
