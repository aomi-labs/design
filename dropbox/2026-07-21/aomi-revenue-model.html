Where the money actually is

The lever mix barely moves year to year — it's outcome-dominant from the start. What changes is whether we can collect it: the flow-based outcome take is the franchise, but part of it waits on a writer that doesn't exist yet.

$23.2M· 73%
Collectable with today's writers

Hosting, model margin, and first-party wallet outcome — the wallet captures its flow on-chain directly (Aomi is the beneficiary), no reserved take needed.

$8.4M· 27%
Behind a reserved writer

The third-party outcome take (vault + bot) and the tool take. Real revenue in the model, uncollectable until those statement_entries subjects ship a writer.

Put plainly: the single highest-leverage engineering action in this whole projection is wiring the third-party outcome-take writer. It converts a quarter of the Year-5 model from "designed" to "banked," and it's the lever most aligned with the one tailwind that compounds.

So what

The structural bet

Four things the model says that a spreadsheet total won't tell you on its own.

1
Outcome is the franchise — 69% of Year-5.
Flow-based, take-rate sticky, and it rides crypto adoption rather than fighting AI deflation. It's the only lever where our growth target (flow ↑) and a fixed policy (30% take) compound in the same direction. Everything else is a supporting act.
2
Model margin is a trap — 8% and shrinking as a share.
Turns grow 260×, yet model margin crawls to $2.7M because $/token falls 3× and BYOK rises to 55%, eroding it from both sides. Charging for tokens is a treadmill; don't build the business on it.
3
Hosting is the floor — 19%, deflation-proof.
Linear in app count, immune to token/tool price compression, and it's cash today. The bot-platform cohort is a hosting annuity that funds the outcome build-out while the flywheel spins up. Boring, and load-bearing.
4
It's a scale game with one collection gap.
Per-turn ARPU is ~flat (the converging variables cancel), so revenue ≈ scale × flow. Hitting the ARR means hitting the growth targets and shipping the reserved outcome-take writer that unlocks 27% of the model. Product growth and one specific piece of billing infra are the whole story.
