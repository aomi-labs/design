# The message

The canonical statement of what aomi is. Every presentation surface — decks,
landing, info pages, the Telegram blurb — is a FORMAT of this file; when this
file changes, every surface below it needs a migration pass before it can be
called "not wrong." The Core board renders the audience sections as a composer:
core line always, tails picked per audience.

## Core line — always

> Aomi Labs builds the native harness on blockchains.

## Audience — retail

Aomi turns plain English into on-chain action: trading bots, prediction-market
agents, DeFi assistants — multi-chain, multi-wallet, ready in one chat. Your
agent watches the market and executes for you; every transaction is simulated
before it is signed, and your keys never leave your hands. Think Claude Code
and a lightweight OpenClaw, on chain.

## Audience — business · hosting

Aomi is the harness that hosts onchain agents — high performance, low cost,
universal protocol execution. As a cloud service, Aomi hosts agentic
applications like trading bots, prediction-market agents, and DeFi assistants
for developers and SaaS partners. You own your frontend, your APIs as tools,
and your wallets — let us be the host of where your harness lives. The stack
ships with React UI, Telegram & Discord integrations, and a Rust SDK.

## Audience — business · transaction processing

Aomi's runtime transacts against arbitrary protocols with a non-custodial
workflow: transactions built from natural language, type-checked at compile
time, simulated before they are signed, with account abstraction and
multi-chain multi-wallet support. x402 pricing and MPP settlement come out of
the box — price, charge, and settle app fees on aomi rails without building
payments infrastructure.
