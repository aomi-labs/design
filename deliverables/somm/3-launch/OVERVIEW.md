# [Partnership] Somm × aomi launch

> Projection of [design#3](https://github.com/aomi-labs/design/issues/3) — the issue is
> canonical; edit there, not here.

Co-branded launch bundle for the Somm.Finance × aomi partnership. Somm ships app-level
fees on aomi rails (x402 pricing + MPP settlement); this bundle is the marketing surface
for the launch: the co-branded video production (architecture animation, launch section,
final-cut insert/outro) and the X post announcing the partnership.

**Contains**

- `video/` — the co-branded launch video production: animated architecture scenes
  (screen-recorded into the final cut), launch section, and the Somm-branded
  insert/outro final-cut pieces.
- `x-post/` — the launch announcement post for X (markdown, screenshot-ready).

**Angle**

Somm is the proof that a DeFi partner can price, charge, and settle app fees on aomi
without building payments infrastructure — the video shows how it works, the post says
it shipped.
