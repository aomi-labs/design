Most AI agents can talk. Very few can safely touch money.

At Shape Rotator Day in New York, Aomi showed why. The demo day was run by IC3, Flashbots, The Convent, and Blockchain Builders, in a room full of crypto and AI researchers.

The idea is simple. Building an agent that moves real money usually takes a team wiring up wallets, signing, and safety checks. Aomi gives you one tool that does all of it. Bring your API, ship it as an agent, and every transaction runs in a simulation first. If it looks wrong, it never gets signed. You keep your keys the whole time.

One harness. Many apps. Every chain.
