Left · 12″ · the why

The problem

Getting software to act on-chain from natural language is slow and dangerous. Teams hand-wire an LLM to wallets and RPC endpoints, ship custodial glue code, and still can't guarantee a transaction does what the user asked.

Who it's for

Teams building agentic applications on crypto — DeFi assistants, agentic trading products, fintech + wallet apps — and the platforms that host many such Apps.

Today's solutions & gaps

  • DIY LLM→wallet glue — custodial, unaudited, no simulation.
  • Generic agent frameworks — not chain-aware, no type safety.
  • Custodial trading products — the user surrenders their keys.

Our approach

Non-custodial by construction. Every action is compile-time type-checked and simulated before it can be signed — an unsafe transaction is impossible to express.
