Cross-cutting recipes to codify

RecipeRule
Button emphasis ramp Pills carry the page-level pair (ink primary + neutral outline); rounded-lg carries the in-flow pair (blue solid + blue outline). Ghost = text-only, link = navigate.
Dismissal Every X is a circle; its size says what it dismisses. 32px with a surface-2 fill closes a surface — modal, sheet, panel. 20px, transparent until hover, clears a field. Nothing else gets an X.
Radius semantics full pills, chips, segments, avatars · xl (12px) cards, tables, menus · lg (8px) in-card controls
Type scale in practice 9–11px uppercase tracked = micro-labels / overlines · 13px = body & controls · mono for all numerals, addresses, amounts. Interactive elements climb three steps: badge 10px (a label, not a target), chip and segment pill together at 12px (a chip and a pill are the same object in different containers), button 13px (the loudest thing you can press). Header controls all stand 32px tall — icon buttons and the theme switch alike.
Surface stack bg → surface → surface-2 → raised, with surface-2 doubling as the sunken track behind segmented controls and chips; hover and accent-subtle are the state fills — flat, no shadows
Tinted surfaces accent-tint fills, accent-outline borders, overlay-border for menu and tooltip hairlines. No raw /10-style opacities in component code.

Gaps · all closed

  • ClosedFocus ring. --aomi-ring now exists and a single :focus-visible rule lives in the widget theme, wrapped in :where() so it adds no specificity and any component can still override it. Nothing falls through to the browser's default outline.
  • ClosedAccent gradient. "Sign to authorize" is the flat blue solid in the button ramp. No gradients remain anywhere.
  • ClosedAlpha tints. The recurring opacities are tokens: --aomi-accent-tint (fills), --aomi-accent-outline (borders), --aomi-overlay-border (menu and tooltip hairlines). 15 ad-hoc /10, /40, /50, /30 utilities were replaced; none remain in the redesigned surfaces.
  • ClosedSelection has one grammar, split by size rather than by component. Pill-sized controls take the solid accent fill (segments, filter chips). Card- and row-sized selection takes accent-subtle with an accent icon or indicator (mode cards, nav rows, menu rows, modal rows) — a solid fill at that scale is a wall of colour. Neutral greys no longer signal "chosen" anywhere.
  • ClosedThe shadcn seam. The aomi-* tokens now live in @aomi-labs/widget-lib (themes/default.css), so every consumer resolves them identically and the portal no longer keeps its own copy. The session panel and thread list speak aomi-* throughout — no muted-foreground, bg-muted/60 or border-border/30 left in either file.
  • ClosedThe registry surfaces are refitted. The chat composer is on №12 — rounded-2xl on bg-aomi-surface, 13px field, 32px ink send circle. The wallet sheet is on the №15 shell: flat 50% scrim with no blur, bg-aomi-raised panel at rounded-2xl, no shadow or ring, and the standard 32px circular close. Its interior rows are still shadcn-vocabulary and are the next sweep — the shell is what №15 defines.
